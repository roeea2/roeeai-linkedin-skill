2. A peer-reviewed 2025 trial found an AI tutor outperformed classroom learning by 0.73-1.3 standard deviations - and students finished 11 minutes faster. That's not incremental.

3. AI cut IEP preparation time by 90% for special ed teachers. In one case, it discovered a student with cortical visual impairment responded better to auditory cues - a breakthrough that improved comprehension in a single week.

4. 81% of educators lack time to build an AI curriculum. 75% lack the knowledge. These aren't failing teachers - they're unsupported ones.

5. The market grows from $7.6B to $112B by 2034. Institutions are not adapting at that pace.
