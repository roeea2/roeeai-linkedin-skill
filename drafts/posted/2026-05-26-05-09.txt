An AI tutor just outperformed traditional classroom instruction in a peer-reviewed RCT - with an effect size up to 1.3 standard deviations. That's not a pilot. That's a signal.

1. 86% of students already use AI for their studies. Schools are catching up to students, not leading them.
2. The RCT result matters: AI tutors cut time-on-task from 60 to 49 minutes while beating in-class outcomes. Less time, better results.
3. Special education breakthrough: AI reduced IEP preparation time by 90% - and in one documented case, identified a student's preferred learning modality in a week that teachers had missed.
4. The gap is teacher training: 55% of teachers report improved outcomes with AI, but under half received any guidance from their institutions. Tools deployed, knowledge withheld.
5. 83% of institutions plan AI teaching assistant deployment by end of 2026 - with or without the readiness to use them well.

I teach high schoolers about AI and build agentic systems. The pattern repeats: the tool is not the problem. The missing mental model is.
