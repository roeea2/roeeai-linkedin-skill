AI is automating 40% of retail inventory decisions in 2026. Most store managers still don't know it is happening.

1. Walmart's AI replenishment system reduced overstock by 32% and stockouts by 41% in 2025 — without a single human approval in the loop for routine orders.

2. Dynamic pricing AI now adjusts prices 3,000 times per day in major retail chains. A human pricing team would manage maybe 50 changes per week.

3. Customer churn prediction models in retail now hit 87% accuracy 30 days out. Stores that act on these predictions retain 22% more high-value customers.

4. Cashierless stores expanded to 8,000 locations globally in 2025. Shrinkage dropped by 60% compared to traditional checkout models.
