92% of students used AI in 2025. A year earlier it was 66%.

That's not a trend - that's a generation already rewired.

1. A Harvard study found AI tutors helped students learn 2x as much in less time. Not marginal gains - 2x.

2. AI reading companions now improve comprehension to the same degree as a human adult. For kids without access to private tutoring, that's a real equity shift.

3. AI cut IEP prep time by 90%. AI-assisted goals scored 9.1-10 on quality metrics vs. 5.5-9.2 without AI. Faster AND measurably better.

4. 81% of educators are optimistic about AI in education. Only 30% of parents say it had a positive impact on their kids. That gap is a communication failure, not an AI failure.

5. 68% of urban teachers have had zero AI training - yet weekly users reclaim 6 hours per week. 6 extra weeks per school year. The tool is here. The training isn't.
