## Tone Characteristics
- Analytical: data, frameworks, numbered breakdowns
- Direct: no fluff, no hollow openers, no "excited to share"
- Technical depth + business context in the same breath
- Speaks from 20 years of real-world experience, not theory
- First person, confident, occasionally challenges conventional wisdom
- Minimal hashtags (2-3 max), no emojis unless truly needed

## Writing Anti-Patterns (never do these)
- "I'm excited to share..."
- "The future of X is..."
- Generic claims without evidence
- More than 3 hashtags
- Emojis as decoration
- Long/em dash (—) — use a regular dash (-) instead
